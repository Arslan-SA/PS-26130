# UdyamSetu AI — Industrial Regulatory Domain Glossary

| Term / Acronym | Full Form | Domain Description |
|---|---|---|
| **MSME** | Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises | Enterprises classified by investment in plant & machinery and annual turnover under MSMED Act 2006. |
| **CTE** | Consent to Establish | Statutory environmental clearance obtained from SPCB before breaking ground on any industrial manufacturing unit. |
| **CTO** | Consent to Operate | Mandatory operating clearance granted by SPCB prior to commissioning or trial production. |
| **SPCB** | State Pollution Control Board | Statutory authority governing Water & Air Pollution control acts and Hazardous Waste rules. |
| **DISH** | Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health | State department regulating factory safety, worker welfare, and plant layouts under the Factories Act 1948. |
| **NOC** | No Objection Certificate | Formal statutory clearance issued by authorities like Fire & Emergency Services, Forest Dept, or Airport Authority. |
| **EPR** | Extended Producer Responsibility | Mandatory registration for manufacturers, importers, and brand owners handling plastic, e-waste, or battery packaging. |
| **Pollution Category** | Red / Orange / Green / White | CPCB industrial classification based on Pollution Index score determining rigor of environmental scrutiny. |
| **Single Window System** | SWS / NSWS | Integrated government portal for statutory business registrations and clearance tracking. |
| **SLA** | Service Level Agreement | Legally prescribed timeframe under Right to Public Services Act for departmental clearance decisions. |
| **Query / Deficiency** | Departmental Scrutiny Note | Structured clarification or document correction requested by a scrutinizing officer halting SLA clock. |
